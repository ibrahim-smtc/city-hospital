## Frequently asked questions

## What Does a Gastroenterologist Treat?

Our specialists treat a wide range of gastrointestinal issues, including acid reflux (GERD), IBS, IBD, ulcers, liver diseases, pancreatitis, gallbladder issues, and colorectal disorders.

## What Are The Risks Associated With Gastroenterology Treatments?

Gastroenterology treatments carry some risks, including infection, bleeding, gastrointestinal perforation, and adverse reactions to anesthesia or medications. Complications can also arise from underlying conditions or medical history. It's important to discuss these risks with the best Gastroenterology treatment provider before undergoing any procedure or treatment.

## When should I consult a gastroenterologist?

You should seek a consultation with a gastroenterologist if you experience persistent digestive symptoms like abdominal pain, diarrhoea, constipation, difficulty swallowing, or rectal bleeding.

## What Procedures or Tests Can a Gastroenterologist Perform?

Gastroenterologists can perform procedures/tests such as endoscopy, colonoscopy, liver biopsy, esophageal manometry, and Capsule Endoscopy to diagnose and treat gastrointestinal conditions.
