# Colposcopy Clinic & Pre-Cancerous lesion Treatment

At Cytecare we strive towards cancer prevention and early detection. As part of the gynaecological oncology speciality, our colposcopy services are focused on investigating abnormalities in the cervix and timely treatment of any pre-cancerous conditions. Colposcopy is a helpful investigation when women have an abnormal Pap test.

## COLPOSCOPY CLINIC & PRE-CANCEROUS LESION TREATMENT

Our [gynaecological oncologists](https://cytecare.com/our-cancer-specialist/), trained in colposcopy performs a thorough vaginal and cervical examination and incorporates a multidisciplinary approach to diagnosis and treatment of cervical abnormalities & pre-cancerous conditions. We provide the full spectrum of diagnostic and therapeutic options for women with abnormal Pap smears or related issues. Under our colposcopy services we offer testing, treatment and follow-up as well as patient support and information.

## Services

Assessment & Treatment of Abnormal Pap/Pre-Cancerous Conditions:

- Cervical Dysplasia
- Squamous cell abnormalities /SIL (Squamous intraepithelial lesion)

1. LGSIL (Low-grade squamous intraepithelial lesion)
2. HSIL (High-grade intra epithelial Lesion)
3. ASC- Atypical squamous cells

- VAIN (Vaginal Intra epithelial neoplasia)
- VIN (Vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia)
