## Frequently asked questions

## What Conditions Does a Urologist Treat?

A urologist treats conditions related to the urinary tract and male reproductive system. This includes issues like urinary tract infections (UTIs), kidney stones, prostate problems, erectile dysfunction, and bladder issues. They also diagnose and treat urological cancers, such as bladder, kidney, and prostate cancer.

## How To Get Rid of a UTI?

To get rid of a UTI (urinary tract infection), it's crucial to drink plenty of water to flush out bacteria. Cranberry juice or supplements may also help. Antibiotics prescribed by a healthcare professional are often necessary to fully clear the infection. Avoiding irritants like caffeine, alcohol, and spicy foods can also promote healing. If symptoms persist or worsen, seek medical attention promptly. If symptoms persist or worsen, seek medical attention promptly, and consider consulting the Best Urology Hospital in Bangalore for specialized care.

## What is the Difference Between a Nephrologist and a Urologist?

A nephrologist is a medical doctor who specializes in kidney care and treating diseases of the kidneys, while a urologist is a surgical specialist who focuses on the urinary tract system and male reproductive system.

## How Long Does it Take To Recover from Hypospadias Surgery?

Recovery from hypospadias surgery typically takes about 4 to 6 weeks. During this time, it's important to follow your doctor's post-operative care instructions closely to ensure proper healing. Avoiding strenuous activities and keeping the surgical area clean and dry can help promote a smooth recovery process.
