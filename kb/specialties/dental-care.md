## Frequently asked questions

## What Is The Best Way To Whiten Your Teeth?

To achieve whiter teeth, consider professional treatments like in-office whitening or at-home kits with dentist supervision. These methods use peroxide-based bleaching agents to remove stains and lighten tooth colour. Regular dental cleanings can also help remove surface stains. Additionally, practising good oral hygiene, like brushing and flossing regularly, can prevent stains and maintain whiteness. Avoiding foods and beverages that stain teeth, like coffee and red wine, can also help maintain a bright smile.

## What Are Sealants?

Sealants are thin, plastic coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth to prevent cavities. They fill in the crevices and grooves where food particles and bacteria often hide, providing a protective barrier against decay. Sealants are commonly used in children and teenagers, as their developing teeth are particularly vulnerable to cavities.

## What Is Fluoride?

Fluoride is a naturally occurring mineral found in water sources and certain foods. It is commonly added to toothpaste and drinking water to help prevent tooth decay and strengthen enamel. Fluoride works by remineralizing the teeth, making them more resistant to acid attacks from plaque bacteria and sugars in the mouth.

## What To Do For Bad Breath?

To address bad breath or halitosis, focus on good oral hygiene: brush your teeth twice daily, floss daily, and clean your tongue. Stay hydrated, avoid odour-causing foods, and consider sugar-free gum or mints. Persistent bad breath may indicate an underlying issue; consult your dentist or healthcare provider for evaluation and treatment.
