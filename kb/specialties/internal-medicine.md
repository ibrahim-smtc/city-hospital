# Internal Medicine

Our dedicated team of board-certified physicians combine extensive medical knowledge with personalised attention. From preventive care and routine check-ups to managing complex conditions, we are here to guide you on your path to well-being. Experience compassionate, patient-centred care at Cytecare, where your health is put first.

Our gynaecology specialists provide treatments for female health problems such as Total
Laparoscopic Hysterectomy, Total Abdominal Hysterectomy, Pap smear and Colposcopy, Urinary
Incontinence treatment, Endometriosis treatment, Fibroid treatment, Infections (UTI, Pelvic
Inflammatory Disease) treatment,Vaginal Hysterectomy, etc. Here, we provide the right medical
care for your gynaec problems by the top gynaecologist. Being an organ-site focused hospital,
our team comprises dedicated gynaecologists, medical experts, pathologists and radiation
therapists who have years of experience and clinical expertise with gynaecological problems.

The division of the gynaecological department is supported by state-of-the-art technology and
care, which drastically improves gynaecological diagnosis and treatment approach for gynaec
patients. The comprehensive services of gynaecology provided by the unit also include surgical
management of benign, but complex conditions, (fibroids, ovarian mass and cyst) and an evolving
field in gynaecology that deals with restoring and preserving fertility during and after the
gynaecological treatment.
