## Frequently asked questions

## What Are Usually The First Signs Of Multiple Sclerosis?

The first signs of multiple sclerosis (MS) may include vision problems, numbness or tingling, weakness, dizziness, coordination difficulties, pain, muscle spasms, and cognitive issues. For accurate diagnosis and treatment, consult Cytecare, the leading neurology hospital in Bangalore.

## How Do I Know If My Headache Is A Migraine?

To determine if your headache is a migraine, look for throbbing pain, often on one side of the head, accompanied by symptoms like nausea, vomiting, and sensitivity to light or sound. Migraines can last 4 to 72 hours if untreated and may be triggered by certain foods, stress, or hormonal changes. A family history of migraines can also increase your risk. If you experience these symptoms regularly, consult Cytecare, the leading neurology hospital in Bangalore, for an accurate diagnosis.

## What Are The Most Common Neurological Disorders?

Neurological disorders, such as epilepsy, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, and migraine, affect the brain, spinal cord, and nerves. They vary in severity and symptoms, all involving nervous system dysfunction. It's crucial to consult a healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.

## What Are The Neurological Examinations?

Neurological examinations are tests that assess nervous system function, aiding in diagnosing brain, spinal cord, and nerve conditions. They include evaluations of mental status, cranial nerves, motor function, coordination, and sensory function, often conducted by neurologists to diagnose and treat neurological disorders.

## When should I see a neurologist?

You should consult a neurologist if you experience frequent headaches, seizures, dizziness, memory loss, numbness, or difficulty with movement or balance.
