## Frequently asked questions

## What Causes Nosebleeds?

Nosebleeds, or epistaxis, often result from the delicate blood vessels in the nose breaking and bleeding. Common causes include dry air, nose picking, trauma, high blood pressure, allergies, sinus infections, and certain medications. Rarely, underlying conditions like blood clotting disorders or nasal tumors can lead to nosebleeds. Preventive measures include staying hydrated, using saline nasal sprays, and avoiding nose-picking. Consult the best hospital for ENT care in Bangalore, if nosebleeds are frequent or severe.

## What Are The Different Types of Hearing Tests?

Hearing tests, or audiometric tests, evaluate hearing ability and diagnose hearing loss. They include pure-tone and speech audiometry, tympanometry, otoacoustic emissions, auditory brainstem response, auditory steady-state response, and behavioural tests. These tests are crucial for understanding hearing health, diagnosing issues, and recommending interventions. Regular testing is important, especially with any changes in hearing ability.

## What Causes Nasal Obstruction?

Nasal obstruction can be caused by various factors, including structural issues like deviated septum or nasal polyps, which block airflow. Inflammation due to allergies or infections, such as sinusitis, can also contribute. Other causes may include enlarged turbinates, adenoids, or tumors. Proper diagnosis by the best hospital for ENT care in Bangalore is crucial for effective treatment.

## What is Cochlear Implant Surgery?

Cochlear implant surgery is a procedure to help people with severe hearing loss. It involves implanting a device in the inner ear to stimulate the auditory nerve, bypassing damaged parts. The device converts sound into electrical signals, allowing recipients to hear and understand speech better.

## What does an ENT do at the first appointment?

During the first appointment with an ENT specialist, patients can expect a thorough evaluation of their ear, nose, and throat health. The ENT will review the medical history, discuss symptoms, and conduct a physical exam.
