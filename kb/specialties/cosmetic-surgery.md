## Frequently asked questions

## Is Cosmetic Surgery Good Or Bad?

Whether cosmetic surgery is good or bad depends on individual circumstances, motivations, and outcomes. While it can enhance self-confidence and correct physical imperfections, it also carries risks and may not always deliver the desired results. It's crucial to thoroughly research procedures, choose qualified surgeons, and have realistic expectations.

## Difference Between Cosmetic And Reconstructive Surgery?

Cosmetic surgery focuses on enhancing aesthetic appearance through elective procedures like breast augmentation and rhinoplasty, usually not covered by insurance. In contrast, reconstructive surgery aims to restore function and appearance after injury or illness, including procedures like scar revision and breast reconstruction, often covered by insurance if deemed medically necessary.

## How Long Does Liposuction Last?

Liposuction results can be long-lasting if you maintain a stable weight. The fat cells removed during the procedure do not grow back. However, it's important to follow a healthy diet and exercise regimen to prevent new fat accumulation in the treated areas.

## What Is The Riskiest Cosmetic Surgery?

The riskiest cosmetic surgery varies depending on individual health factors and the specific procedure involved. Generally, surgeries with higher complication rates include those involving multiple procedures, extensive tissue manipulation, or surgeries on sensitive areas like the face or genitals. Before undergoing any cosmetic procedure, it is imperative to look for Reconstructive Cosmetic Surgery in Bangalore, thoroughly comprehend the associated risks, and carefully consider them in comparison to the potential benefits.
