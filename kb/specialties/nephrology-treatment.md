## Frequently asked questions

## What conditions are treated at Cytecare Humanist Institute of Dialysis and Renal Disease?

Chronic kidney diseases, acute kidney injury, renal diseases, polycystic kidney disease, autoimmune disease, vasculitis, etc

## What is kidney failure?

Kidney failure is also called End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD). It is the final stage of chronic kidney disease in which the kidneys will stop functioning.

## What is Dialysis?

Dialysis is a medical procedure for patients with kidney failure. Dialysis artificially filters waste products, excess fluids and electrolytes from blood.

## Early signs & symptoms of kidney disease?

Foamy Urine, Blood in urine, increased frequency of urination, swelling in legs, ankles or face, fatigue & weakness, etc.

## What is hemodiafiltration?

Hemodialfiltration, Also known as HDF, is a form of renal replacement therapy which is used to treat patients with kidney failure. This procedure combines elements of both hemodialysis and hemofiltration, which aims to remove toxins and fluids from the blood.

## Advantages of hemodiafiltration?

Some of the major benefits of hemodiafiltration include comprehensive removal of toxins and fluids from blood, reduced inflammation, improved fluid management, improved cardiovascular health, and better control of uremic toxins.
