# Dr Vasudha C K

![Dr Vasudha C K](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Dr-Vasudha-C-K-cover.jpg)

Head - International Business

Dr. Vasudha C K, holds a bachelor’s degree from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences and a Master’s in Hospital Administration from Manipal University. She began her career at HCG Hospitals, Bangalore, in the International Marketing department and went on to gain extensive experience in International Patient Services at Hosmat Hospitals and Manipal Hospitals. Through her professional journey, she has worked closely with international patients, focusing on coordination of care and ensuring a seamless patient experience.
