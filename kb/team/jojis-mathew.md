# Jojis Mathew

![Jojis Mathew](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Jojis-cover.jpg)

Centre Head

Jojis Mathew is the Centre Head at Cytecare Hospitals with over 21 years of experience in hospital operations, strategic growth, and P&L management. He holds a B. Tech from Calicut University and an MBA from Christ Institute of Management, Bangalore. He has held leadership roles across reputed healthcare organizations at Manipal Health Systems, Manipal Health Enterprises, Motherhood Hospital, Cloudnine Hospitals, HCG MSR Hospitals, HCG Hospital, and Ramaiah Memorial Hospital, Bangalore.

At Cytecare Hospitals, Jojis oversees operations, service delivery, and financial growth, ensuring operational excellence and patient-centric outcomes.
