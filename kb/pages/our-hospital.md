## Infection control

At Cytecare, we adhere to standards of infection-control prescribed by the NABH (National
Accreditation Board of Hospitals) and use HEPA filters which keep the environment dust and
allergen-free. Our sterilisation design incorporates the use of laminar flow/plenum to maintain
the quality of air ventilation and minimise airborne contaminants.

To secure high efficiency for
infection control, we have acquired a high-end, advanced Central Sterilisation Supply Department
(CSSD), which enables the efficient and seamless flow of sterile goods.

### PET CT

Advances in diagnostic technology together with skilled clinical and technician expertise are
necessary for early and accurate detection of cancer. This is critical for determining the way
forward with respect to treatment and therapy.

PET CT is one of the most effective diagnostic techniques that identify the primary location of
the origin of cancer. Cytecare has a state-of-the art PET CT, the GE Discovery IQ 3 Ring16 slice
PET with LightBurst technology which reduces scan time and provides superior scan quality.

## Digital Mammography with Tomosynthesis (DMT)

Our Digital Breast Tomosynthesis (DBT) is the next generation in breast imaging digital
technology with superior 3D imaging, and a scanning time of less than 20 seconds.

This is
beneficial because patients receive 75% less radiation compared to the radiation from LM-screen
mammograms. Also the accuracy is very high for smaller lesions, as they are displayed with
greater clarity, and potential breast tumours can be detected more effectively.

## Elekta Versa HD Linear Accelerator

Cytecare is the first hospital in the state of Karnataka to install the Elekta Versa HD. This
ground-breaking linear accelerator (or LINAC) advances modern cancer care with the option of
conventional radiology treatment, and the added versatility to deliver sophisticated, targeted
treatments.

The LINAC chamber at the hospital has also been aesthetically designed to create a
serene ambience for patients while they undergo treatment.
