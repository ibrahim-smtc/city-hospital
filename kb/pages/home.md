## Frequently asked questions

Here are the frequently asked questions about cancer diagnosis,
cancer treatment and cancer care.

## What are the early symptoms of cancer and when should i visit the doctor

The signs and symptoms of cancer vary based on the type
and
the stage of cancer. For patients visiting the hospital with complaints like
unexpected fevers, unexplained weight loss, lumps, etc. will be consulted with
the
best cancer specialists.
It is highly advised that you visit your healthcare provider if your symptoms
persist for more than a week. If your consultant suspects cancer, you will be
advised cancer diagnosis tests, after which starts the screening and cancer
treatment plan, which is again customised for each patient depending on the stage
of
cancer and other medical conditions. Get your consultation done with the top
oncologists at the best cancer hospital in Bangalore.

## Is breast cancer treatment available in Bangalore ?

Yes, breast cancer treatment is available in Bangalore.
Cytecare is the leading cancer care hospital in Bangalore with state-of-the-art
facilities providing comprehensive breast cancer treatment services, including
minimally invasive procedures and surgical techniques designed for minimal
postoperative discomfort and issues such as lymphedema.
The holistic evaluation and cancer treatment plan of breast cancer includes
breast
cancer risk assessment and screening, breast imaging, breast cancer surgery,
breast
reconstruction, and support services for women living with breast cancer.

## What are the cancer treatment options available?

The cancer treatments vary based on several factors,
primarily including the type of cancer and its current stage at which it is
diagnosed. Cancer treatment options include surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy,
immunotherapy, etc.
Unlike other diseases, in cancer management, more than one specialist is involved
in
the cancer treatment and the various phases of the disease. Cytecare focuses on
the
solution of a multidisciplinary approach, where an integrated team of cancer
specialists understand and evaluate the patient's journey.

## How does the Cytecare Cancer Hospital follow Covid-19 safety protocols and regulations?

Cytecare has standardized protocols for all our patients,
their attendants, and staff, as protective steps against the COVID-19 global
pandemic (as declared by the WHO). These include medical screening, the use of
PPEs
and sanitization guidelines for all. The best measure is to stay protected. We
care
for all and urge everyone to follow personal hygiene and hygiene in their
surroundings as well.

## What happens if I test positive for Covid during my cancer treatment?

You’ll need to consult your oncologist if and when you
get
tested positive for the Covid-19 virus during your cancer treatment. Do not make
any
decisions by yourself. The top cancer specialists at Bangalore will guide you
through any necessary changes, if needed, in your cancer treatment till you test
negative for the virus.
