## Prevention and Screening

Prevention is better than cure in all senses, when it comes to cancer. The most effective
method of preventing cancer is by steering clear of unhealthy lifestyle habits, which put
you at a higher risk. The second is timely Cancer screening, which helps in detecting cancer
even before the symptoms start to affect normal life. Cancer test is also vital as most
cancers detected early have better prognosis and recovery. Cytecare Cancer Hospital has
developed holistic cancer screening packages for men and women for early detection of the
common cancers found in India.

The Cytecare Women's screening package plus is a complete testing program that helps early
detection of a wide variety of cancers. This covers blood count, peripheral smear,
creatinine, fasting blood sugar, liver function test, lipid profile, FT3, FT4, TSH, HBsAg,
anti-HCV, urine routine & microscopy, Stool Occult Blood, ECG, USG Thyroid, Abdomen &
Pelvic, X-Ray Chest, Pap Smear, Liquid Cytology + HPV DNA, Mammogram, Consultation with
Breast Oncologist, Gynaecologic Oncologist, Dietician, Head & Neck Oncologist,
Gastrointestinal Oncologist.

The Cytecare men's screening package is customized to cover screenings for most common
cancers. It covers the blood count, peripheral smear, creatinine, fasting blood sugar, liver
function test, Lipid profile FT3, FT4, TSH, HBsAg, Anti-HCV, Urine Routine & Microscopy,
Stool Occult Blood, ECG, USG Thyroid, Abdomen & Pelvic, X-Ray Chest, PSA (Prostate-Specific
antigen), Dietician, Head & Neck Oncologist, Medical Oncologist.

We, at Cytecare, understand how the habit of smoking lures in its victims and tightens a
noose of fatal diseases on them. It causes cancers of the lung, oesophagus, larynx, mouth,
throat, kidney, bladder, liver, pancreas, stomach, cervix, colon, and rectum, as well as
acute myeloid leukemia. Cytecare's Tobacco Cessation Clinic is built for immediate
implementation, which provides effective guidance to overcome the mental and physical
addiction from tobacco. We also have a dedicated team of counsellors who work to motivate
and encourage the person to build a strong mind to fight through it and never relapse. The
program gives therapy for changing the habitual lifestyle, teaches techniques to manage
stress, and administer medicine when necessary to help in the quitting process.
