## About Cytecare hospitals

Cytecare is the best patient-centric hospital in Bangalore, India, which provides global
standards of healthcare and surgical excellence.

We bring a multi-faceted approach to healthcare through what we call the four pillars of
treatment   
1.Organ-site Focus   
 2.Patient-centric Culture,   
 3.Standardised Global Guidelines
  
 4.Clinical Research.

Our commitment to providing the highest level of care & treatment
to patients has led us to enhance the impact of treatment across
multiple specialities through Cytecare Specialities and provide
highly advanced cancer treatment and surgical care through
our Oncology Center of Excellence.

Cytecare Hospitals is located in Bangalore, India and brings global standards of healthcare accessible to all through the Centers of Excellence
across oncology and other specialities, We believe and know that every cancer is different, and
hence, a specialised cancer treatment is necessary for each.

## Your Right Way to Health

We understand and stress on the right way to health, which gives rise to our multi-faceted approach to healthcare at Cytecare, relying on what we call the four pillars of treatment.

![organ-site](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/themes/cytecare-wp-theme-v2-master/build/img/icons/organ-site.svg)

### Organ-site Focus

At Cytecare, we have a dedicated team of doctors, super-specialised in the medical, clinical & surgical treatments of particular organs.

![Patient-centric](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/themes/cytecare-wp-theme-v2-master/build/img/icons/patient-centric.svg)

### Patient-centric Culture

We focus on holistic wellness, rooted in empathy & compassion, which improves clinical outcomes by addressing patients' needs, providing emotional support, counseling, and ancillary services for patients and their families.

![Global-Guidelines](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/themes/cytecare-wp-theme-v2-master/build/img/icons/global-guidelines.svg)

### Standardised Global Guidelines

We provide care based on, and drawn from, globally recognised, gold standard guidelines such as, NCCN, ESMO, NHS, ICMR and Tata Memorial Hospital.

![Clinical-Research](https://cytecare.com/wp-content/themes/cytecare-wp-theme-v2-master/build/img/icons/clinical-research.svg)

### Clinical Research

Cytecare is focused on clinical research to ensure continuous,better clinical outcomes and gives rise to an evidence-based, scientific approach to treatment.
