## Contribute to our vision to make India a global cancer care destination.

Advances in diagnostic technology together with skilled clinical and technician expertise are
necessary for early and accurate detection of cancer. This is critical for determining the way
forward with respect to treatment and therapy.

PET CT is one of the most effective diagnostic techniques that identify the primary location of
the origin of cancer. Cytecare has a state-of-the art PET CT, the GE Discovery IQ 3 Ring16 slice
PET with LightBurst technology which reduces scan time and provides superior scan quality.
