# DR. BHARGAVI JOIS K S

## Senior Consultant - Nuclear Medicine

[Request Appointment](/appointment?doctor=dr.-bhargavi-jois-k-s&doc-img=https%3A%2F%2Fcytecare.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F02%2FDr-Bhargavi-cover.jpg&designation=Senior Consultant - Nuclear Medicine)

### About me

- **MBBS** from KIMS, Hubli
- **Diploma in Radiation Medicine from Radiation**
  **Medicine Centre**, BARC, Tata Memorial hospital
  annexe, Mumbai
- **DNB** from Jaslok Hospital and research centre,
  Mumbai
- **MNAMS**, New Delhi
  - Fellow of European board of Nuclear Medicine
  (FEBNM) from Barcelona, SPAIN.
  - Fellow of Asian board of Nuclear Medicine
  (FANMB) from Seoul, South Korea.

**Total experience: 15 years**

- Consultant and Head, Nuclear medicine, Zulekha
- Yenepoya institute of Oncology, Yenepoya Medical
  College Hospital, Mangalore.
- Consultant at Manipal Hospitals, Bangalore.
- Consultant at Jaslok hospital and research centre,
  Mumbai.

---

**Areas of Special Interest**

**Advanced Diagnostics:**

– PET-CT including oncology, infection imaging,  
and Neuro-imaging.  
– General nuclear medicine.  
– Nuclear cardiology.

**Targeted Theranostics**

– PRRT and PRLT for the treatment of tumors of  
prostate, neuroendocrine tumors,  
pheochromocytoma with  
Lutetium-177/Actinium-225/Tb-161  
PSMA/DOTATATE/FAPI)  
I-131 MIBG therapy for neuroblastoma.  
– Radio-iodine therapy for differentiated thyroid  
cancer and hyperthyroidism.  
– Bone pain palliation.

**Key achievements**

– Awarded Fellow of Asian board of Nuclear  
Medicine (FANMB) from Seoul, South Korea,  
2022, securing 6th rank among Asia-Oceania  
region.  
– Won the Best Poster Award at the International  
Atomic Energy Association (IAEA) International  
Conference on Integrated Medical Imaging In  
Cardiovascular Diseases, Vienna, 2022.  
– Recipient of travel grants from the  
International Atomic Energy Association  
(IAEA), Vienna and Nuclear Medicine Society,  
Singapore.  
– Served as a DNB faculty and mentored post  
graduate residents at Manipal hospitals and  
Yenepoya Medical college and coordinator for  
the BSc. Nuclear Medicine Technology course  
at Yenepoya allied health sciences.  
– Invited speaker at NM conference and  
symposiums. Presented papers and posters in  
several International and national conferences  
and has National and international publications.  
– Reviewer of International indexed journal.

**Certifications and Memberships:**

– Lifetime member of Society of Nuclear medicine  
of India (SNMI) and Association of Nuclear  
Medicine physicians of India (ANMPI).  
– Member of European board of Nuclear Medicine.  
– Certified Radiological Safety Officer (RSO).
