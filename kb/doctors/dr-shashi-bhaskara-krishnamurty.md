# DR SHASHI BHASKARA KRISHNAMURTY

## Director, Critical care and Consultant Intensivist

[Request Appointment](/appointment?doctor=dr-shashi-bhaskara-krishnamurty&doc-img=https%3A%2F%2Fcytecare.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2023%2F06%2FDR-SHASHI-BHASKARA-KRISHNAMURTY.jpg&designation=Director, Critical care and Consultant Intensivist)

### About me

Speciality - FCA, FCICM, MHA
**Total Experience:** 22 years
- Curtin University, Australia
- College of Intensive Care Medicine, Australia and New Zealand
- Royal College of Anaesthesia, Ireland
- Government Medical College, Bellary

---

**Areas of Special Interest**  
– Neuro intensive care  
– General intensive care  
– Critical care education for team performance  
– Improving access to critical care

**Key Achievements**  
– Over 20 years Intensive care at centers of excellence across three countries UK, Australia, and India  
– Master of Health Administration from Curtin University, Australia  
– Clinical Advisor and Course Director at the Institute of Health Management and Research, Bangalore  
– Establishing a critical care education platform for frontline critical care teams in places where trained specialists and academics are not the norm  
– Course director of Integrated Digital Intensive Care Management, helping to empower frontline critical care teams with core critical care, technology and management knowledge and skills  
– Instructor for critical care education like the BASIC courses from Hong Kong & Australia, life support courses by American Heart Association

**Membership**  
– Karnataka Medical Council  
– Australian Medical Council  
– General Medical Council, UK  
– Indian Society of Intensive Care  
– Australia and New Zealand Society of Intensive Care

**Publications**  
– Authored a chapter in “1000 MCQs in Intensive Care” book  
– “Operational model to increase intensive care unit telemedicine capacity rapidly during a pandemic: experience in India.” British Journal of Anaesthesia 128.6 (2022): e343-e345  
– Principle Investigator of a participating organization – GLIMP study published in the Lancet, HERMES study by ISCCM
