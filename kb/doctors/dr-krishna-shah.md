**Areas of Expertise:**  
– Management of neurosurgical emergencies and elective brain & spine surgeries.  
– Advanced microscopic and endoscopic neurosurgical procedures.  
– Brain tumor surgeries.  
– Spine surgeries and minimally invasive spine procedures.  
– Skull base surgeries.  
– Endoscopic cranial and spinal procedures.  
– CSF leak repair procedures.  
– Management of back pain, headache, and neurological disorders.  
– Neurotrauma management.  
– Academic teaching and surgical training of residents and medical students.  
– Clinical research and publication in neurosurgery.

**Achievements:**  
– Dr. A. D. Sehgal Award for Best PG Student in Neurosurgery – Sir Ganga Ram Hospital (2019).  
– Certificate of Commendation for Thesis – Endoscopic Transcranial Repair of CSF Rhinorrhea.  
– Third Prize – Innovative Technique to Prevent VP Shunt Block, Neuroendocon – 2022.  
– Extensive experience in microscopic and endoscopic neurosurgical techniques.  
– Contributor to OpenAI HealthBench evaluation and AI safety initiatives in healthcare.

**Membership:**

– Participated in World Neurosciences Summit, Delhi.
