**MBBS** – M.S. Ramaiah Medical College, Bangalore

**Total Years of Experience**: 18+ years

**Areas of Special Interest:**  
– Precision Radiation Oncology  
– Stereotactic Radiosurgery (SRS)  
– Stereotactic Body Radiotherapy (SBRT)  
– Image Guided Radiotherapy (IGRT)  
– Intensity Modulated Radiotherapy (IMRT)  
– VMAT & Adaptive Radiotherapy  
– CyberKnife Radiosurgery  
– Brachytherapy  
– Gynecological Malignancies  
– Bone Marrow Transplant Radiation Support  
– Benign Conditions treated with Radiotherapy

**Key Achievements:**  
– Over 18 years of experience in comprehensive cancer care.  
– Expertise in advanced radiotherapy techniques from conventional therapy to modern precision oncology.  
– Faculty & Thesis Guide for DNB Radiation Oncology.  
– Former In-charge HOD – B.Sc Radiotherapy Technology (RTT).  
– Academic mentor for postgraduate oncology trainees.  
– Experience in CT/MRI/PET Fusion-based treatment planning.  
– Leadership in multidisciplinary oncology management.

**Publications & Research:**  
– Prospective evaluation of localized and locally advanced carcinoma prostate treated with IGRT and SBRT.  
– Research on immunological changes in CyberKnife radiosurgery patients.  
– Six Sigma analytical study for process improvement in Radiation Oncology.  
– Participation and presentations in national and international oncology conferences.

**Awards & Recognition:**

– Scientific Committee Head – Hyperthermiacon 2023.  
– Hon. Secretary – Indian Medical Association Central Branch, Bangalore.  
– Life Member – Association of Radiation Oncologists of India.  
– Life Member – European Society for Medical Oncology.  
– Active participant in AROICON, Indian Cancer Congress & advanced oncology workshops.
