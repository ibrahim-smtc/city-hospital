# Kidney Screening Clinic

Kidney health is becoming an increasingly important concern as more individuals across different age groups experience lifestyle-related and metabolic changes that can silently impact kidney function. If you are unsure about your kidney health, undergoing a routine kidney screening is a crucial step toward early detection. Factors such as diabetes, hypertension, dehydration, long-term medication use and family history significantly increase the risk. The purpose of kidney screening clinic is to identify the early stages of kidney issues, which can lead to chronic kidney disease, acute kidney injury, and even progressive loss of kidney function.

India is witnessing a steady rise in kidney disorders, with over 200,000 new patients requiring dialysis each year, underscoring the increasing national burden and the importance of early evaluation and prevention.

For accurate evaluation and specialised guidance, Cytecare’s Kidney Screening Clinic in Bangalore offers advanced diagnostics, kidney function assessments, and personalised monitoring plans designed to detect issues early and support long-term renal health.
