A COPD clinic is a specialized medical facility or department that focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and management of Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD). It is a progressive lung condition characterized by airflow limitation, making it difficult for individuals to breathe.

We offer a multidisciplinary approach to COPD care, involving various healthcare professionals who specialize in pulmonary medicine, respiratory therapy, nutrition, exercise physiology, and mental health support. #### Who Should Attend ?

- Chronic Cough – Cough more than 2-3 weeks
- Gradually increasing Breathlessness associated with Wheezing
- Chest Pain/Chest Heaviness
- Cough associated with excessive Phlegm or Blood
- Weight Loss and Muscle Wasting in Chronic Smokers
- Smokers with Sleep Disturbances
- Chronic Smokers with more than 10 years of smoking history
- Family history of Lung Cancer or Bronchitis
