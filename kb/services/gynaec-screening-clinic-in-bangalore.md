# Gynaec Screening Clinic

Women’s gynaec health is gaining increasing attention as more individuals across different age groups experience concerns that may impact long-term wellbeing. If you are unsure about your reproductive or gynaec health, consider undergoing a routine gynaecological screening. Factors such as hormonal changes, lifestyle habits, age, and family history play a significant role. Unaddressed issues can lead to conditions like fibroids, cervical abnormalities, ovarian cysts, and various gynaec cancers.

In India, the incidence of female reproductive-organ cancers remains substantial: for example, breast and cervical cancers together account for roughly 40% of all cancers in Indian women.
